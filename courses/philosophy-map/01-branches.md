## Traditions

- **Analytic** (Anglo-American): logic, clarity, language, science.
- **Continental** (European): history, experience, power — phenomenology (Husserl, Heidegger), existentialism (Sartre, de Beauvoir, Camus), critical theory, Foucault, Derrida.
- **Non-Western**: Confucianism, Daoism, Buddhist philosophy (no-self, dependent origination), Indian logic (Nyaya), Islamic philosophy (Avicenna, Averroes).
