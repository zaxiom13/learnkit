## Physics

| question | positions |
|---|---|
| **What is time?** | presentism (only now is real) vs eternalism / **block universe** (relativity's relativity of simultaneity pushes many physicists here) |
| **Arrow of time** | laws near time-symmetric; the arrow comes from the low-entropy past (Boltzmann, the past hypothesis) |
| **Space** | substantivalism (Newton: space is a thing) vs relationalism (Leibniz, Mach: only relations) — GR's hole argument revived it |
| **Probability in QM** | frequencies? propensities? credences? branch weights in many-worlds? |
| **Laws of nature** | regularities (Humean) vs necessities |
| **Fine-tuning** | constants seem tuned for life: design, multiverse + anthropic selection, or just luck? |

```viz lightcone
> Why relativity pushes people toward the block universe: events outside your light cone have no agreed time order.
```

## Mathematics

| view | maths is… |
|---|---|
| **Platonism** | discovered — abstract objects really exist (Gödel was one) |
| **Formalism** (Hilbert) | a game with symbols and rules |
| **Intuitionism / constructivism** (Brouwer) | mental construction; rejects the law of excluded middle for infinite sets — ties into type theory and proof assistants |
| **Logicism** (Frege, Russell) | reducible to logic (damaged by Russell's paradox and Gödel) |
| **Structuralism** | about structures, not objects |
| **Fictionalism** | a useful fiction |

**Wigner (1960)**: "The unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics in the natural sciences" — why do abstract structures invented for their beauty (complex numbers, group theory, Riemannian geometry) later describe nature exactly?


```reflect
? Why does special relativity push many physicists toward the "block universe"?
- relativity of simultaneity: no universal "now"
- different observers slice spacetime differently
- so past/future events seem equally real
model: In special relativity, whether two distant events are simultaneous depends on your motion, so there's no universal "now" dividing past from future. If a moving observer's "now" includes events in my future, it's hard to say only the present exists, so many conclude all of spacetime is equally real — the block universe.
```
