## Cross-currents

- Japanese prints (**Japonisme**) reshaped Impressionism: flat colour, bold cropping (Van Gogh copied Hiroshige).
- African sculpture shaped Picasso's Cubism (*Les Demoiselles d'Avignon*, 1907).
- Islamic tilings anticipated **quasi-crystalline** patterns (Girih tiles, ~1453 Darb-i Imam shrine) — mathematically akin to Penrose tilings.
