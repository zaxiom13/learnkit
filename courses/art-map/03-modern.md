| movement | idea | names |
|---|---|---|
| **Fauvism** | wild non-natural colour | Matisse |
| **Cubism** | many viewpoints at once; fractured form | Picasso, Braque |
| **Futurism** | speed, machines | Boccioni |
| **Expressionism** | inner emotion, distortion | Munch, Kirchner, Kandinsky |
| **Dada** | anti-art after WWI's absurdity; readymades | **Duchamp** ("Fountain", 1917) |
| **Surrealism** | dreams, the unconscious | Dalí, Magritte, Ernst |
| **Bauhaus** | art + craft + industry; form follows function | Gropius, Klee, Albers |
| **Abstract Expressionism** | gesture and colour fields; NYC becomes centre | Pollock, Rothko, de Kooning |
| **Pop Art** | mass culture, repetition | Warhol, Lichtenstein |
| **Minimalism** | simple industrial forms | Judd, Agnes Martin |
| **Conceptual art** | the idea is the work | Sol LeWitt, Kosuth |
| **Contemporary** | installation, video, performance, identity | Abramović, Kusama, Ai Weiwei, Hirst |
| **Generative / computational** | rules and code make the work | Vera Molnár, Harold Cohen (AARON), Casey Reas; now AI image models |

```viz tree
. Reaction to photography & industry | late 1800s
.. Impressionism → Post-Impressionism | light, then structure and emotion
.. Fauvism (1905) | colour set free
.. Expressionism | inner feeling
. Breaking the picture | 1907–1920s
.. Cubism | Picasso, Braque
... Futurism | speed and machines
... Constructivism | art for revolution
.. De Stijl / Mondrian | pure grids and primaries
. Breaking "art" itself | after WWI
.. Dada | Duchamp's readymades
... Surrealism | the unconscious
... Conceptual art (1960s) | the idea is the work
. After WWII (New York) | 1940s–60s
.. Abstract Expressionism | Pollock, Rothko
.. Pop Art | Warhol
.. Minimalism | Judd, Martin
. Now | 1970s →
.. Performance & installation | Abramović, Kusama
.. Generative & AI art | Molnár → AI models
> Modernism's family tree: each movement reacting to the last.
```

```choice
? Why was Duchamp's "Fountain" (a urinal) so important?
- [x] It argued that art is defined by the artist's choice and context, not craft — opening the door to conceptual art // The "readymade".
- [ ] It was beautifully made
- [ ] It was the first sculpture in porcelain
```

```choice
? Sol LeWitt's wall drawings are written instructions that others execute. Which idea does that anticipate?
- [x] Generative/algorithmic art — the artwork as a program // "The idea becomes a machine that makes the art."
- [ ] Photorealism
- [ ] Rococo decoration
```

```reflect
? Is AI-generated imagery art? Argue using a movement from this lesson.
- uses Duchamp/conceptual/generative precedent
- considers authorship/intention/choice
- takes a position
model: Conceptual and generative art already separated the idea from the hand: LeWitt wrote instructions, Molnár wrote code. By that lineage, what matters is the choices — framing, selection, context. An AI image can be art when someone makes meaningful choices with it; the tool doesn't disqualify it, though uncurated output with no intent is closer to wallpaper.
```
