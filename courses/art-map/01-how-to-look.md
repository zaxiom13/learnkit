## The formal elements

| element | ask |
|---|---|
| **Line** | contour, gesture, direction, energy |
| **Shape & form** | flat or volumetric? geometric or organic? |
| **Colour** | hue, value (light/dark), saturation; warm vs cool; complementary contrasts |
| **Value / chiaroscuro** | light and shadow modelling; **tenebrism** = dramatic dark (Caravaggio) |
| **Space** | linear perspective, atmospheric perspective, flatness |
| **Composition** | rule of thirds, golden ratio (often over-claimed), symmetry, leading lines, negative space |
| **Texture & facture** | brushwork, impasto, surface |
| **Scale** | size relative to the viewer |

## Three questions for any work

1. **What do I see?** (describe before interpreting)
2. **How is it made and arranged?** (the elements above, medium, technique)
3. **What does it mean, and in what context?** (who made it, for whom, when, why — **iconography**, patronage, politics)
