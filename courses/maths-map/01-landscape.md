## Great unifications

- **Descartes**: geometry ↔ algebra (coordinates).
- **Galois**: solving polynomials ↔ symmetry groups (why no quintic formula).
- **Klein's Erlangen programme**: a geometry = a space + a symmetry group.
- **The Langlands programme**: a vast web linking number theory, representation theory and geometry — the "grand unified theory of mathematics". Wiles' proof of **Fermat's Last Theorem** (1994) used one bridge (modularity of elliptic curves).
