| open problem | status |
|---|---|
| **Quantum gravity** | GR and QFT clash at the Planck scale (~10⁻³⁵ m). Candidates: **string theory** (with **AdS/CFT** holography), **loop quantum gravity**, asymptotic safety, causal sets |
| **Dark matter** | WIMPs not found yet; axions, sterile neutrinos, primordial black holes, or modified gravity (MOND struggles with clusters and the CMB) |
| **Dark energy** | cosmological constant problem: naive QFT estimate off by ~10¹²⁰ |
| **Matter–antimatter asymmetry** | known CP violation too small |
| **Neutrino masses** | known to be non-zero (oscillations, Nobel 2015); mechanism and absolute scale unknown; Dirac or Majorana? |
| **Hierarchy problem** | why is gravity so weak / the Higgs mass so light? |
| **Measurement problem** | what *is* collapse? (interpretations lesson) |
| **Arrow of time** | laws are (nearly) time-symmetric; why was early entropy so low? (the "past hypothesis") |
| **Turbulence** | Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness — a Millennium Prize problem |
| **Room-temperature superconductivity** | many false alarms (e.g. LK-99, 2023) |

## Where physicists go in industry

Quant finance (stochastic calculus, Monte Carlo, statistics), ML research, quantum computing companies, semiconductors, climate modelling, data science. The transferable skills: **modelling, approximation, dimensional analysis, orders of magnitude, and knowing when a model is wrong.**
