## The Axial Age (~800–200 BCE)

Karl Jaspers noticed that, in several places at once, thinkers turned to universal ethics and reflection:

| region | figures / traditions |
|---|---|
| Greece | Socrates, Plato, Aristotle; democracy in Athens; Euclid; Archimedes |
| India | the Buddha, the Upanishads, Mahavira (Jainism) |
| China | Confucius, Laozi (Daoism), the "Hundred Schools" |
| Israel/Persia | the Hebrew prophets; Zoroaster (dates debated) |

## Empires

| empire | legacy |
|---|---|
| **Persia (Achaemenid)** | first great multi-ethnic empire; roads, tolerance of local customs |
| **Alexander & Hellenistic kingdoms** | Greek culture spread to Egypt and India; Library of Alexandria |
| **Rome** | law, engineering, Latin; Christianity became state religion (380 CE) |
| **Han China** | Confucian bureaucracy; paper; the Silk Road |
| **Maurya (Ashoka)** | spread Buddhism across Asia |
| **Islamic caliphates** (7th c. →) | House of Wisdom in Baghdad; preserved and advanced Greek science; algebra (al-Khwarizmi → "algorithm"), optics (Ibn al-Haytham) |
| **Mongols** (13th c.) | largest contiguous land empire; linked Eurasia (and spread the Black Death) |
