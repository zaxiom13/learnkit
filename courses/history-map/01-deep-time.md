## The agricultural revolution

Farming allowed **surplus** → **cities** → **specialists** (priests, soldiers, scribes) → **states, taxes, writing** (first used for accounting). Also brought inequality, disease from livestock, and harder labour — which is why some historians (e.g. Jared Diamond, Yuval Harari) have called it a mixed blessing.

## Frameworks for "why did history go this way?"

- **Geography** — *Guns, Germs and Steel* (Diamond): east–west continents spread crops and animals easily.
- **Institutions** — *Why Nations Fail* (Acemoglu & Robinson, Nobel 2024): inclusive vs extractive institutions.
- **Ideas and culture** — Weber, McCloskey: values and rhetoric about commerce and innovation.
- **Energy** — history as the story of capturing more energy (fire → farming → coal → oil → nuclear).
