## Political revolutions

- **American Revolution (1776)**: government by consent; written constitution.
- **French Revolution (1789)**: liberty, equality, fraternity; the Terror; Napoleon; spread of legal codes and nationalism.
- **Haitian Revolution (1791–1804)**: the only successful large-scale slave revolt founding a state.
- **1848**: revolutions across Europe; Marx & Engels' *Communist Manifesto*.

## Industrial revolutions

| wave | technology |
|---|---|
| 1st (~1760–1840, Britain) | steam engine (Watt), textiles, coal, iron, railways |
| 2nd (~1870–1914) | electricity, steel, chemicals, internal combustion, telegraph/telephone |
| 3rd (~1950 →) | electronics, computers, the internet |
| 4th (talked about now) | AI, biotech, automation |

**The Great Divergence**: before ~1800, living standards were similar in the richest parts of Europe and China; after, the West pulled far ahead. Explanations: cheap coal, colonies, institutions, high wages driving labour-saving invention (Robert Allen), a culture of useful knowledge (Joel Mokyr, Nobel 2025).

## Australia in this arc

1788 British First Fleet → dispossession of Aboriginal peoples and frontier wars; gold rushes (1850s) → rapid growth; **Federation 1901**; early democratic firsts (secret ballot, women's suffrage 1902 at federal level); post-WWII mass immigration; end of White Australia policy (1966–73); Mabo decision (1992) overturned "terra nullius".
